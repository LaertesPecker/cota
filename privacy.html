Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Cota is a personal app built and used by one person, its developer. This policy explains what it does with data, including data from Google user accounts.

Data Cota accesses

  • Google Calendar (scopes calendar.readonly and calendar.events): Cota reads event times and titles to find free time, and creates, moves or deletes the training-session events it books itself.
  • Health and activity data from Android Health Connect, plus data you enter in the app: training sessions, habits, check-ins.

How it is used

This data is used only to plan the day, book sessions and send reminders inside Cota. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties. It is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a private Supabase database in the EU (Paris region). The Google refresh token is kept encrypted in Supabase Vault and is never stored on the device. Access is limited to the account owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Cota's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the app is in use. You can revoke Cota's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your data deleted, email the address below.
